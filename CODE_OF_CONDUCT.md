# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Flooding the project with automated or agent-generated issues, comments or pull requests that no human has reviewed.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor.

Report problems to the maintainer privately through GitHub (for example with the Security tab's private reporting, or by contacting the repository owner). All reports are handled confidentially.
